🏏About MCA

Saturday senior competitions and Sunday junior rounds, played across Melbourne through the winter season.

🏏Saturday Cricket

T20 in the mornings, T35 in the afternoons, on a fixture published ahead of the season.

🧑‍⚖️Professional Umpires

Every game has an appointed umpire, some of whom also umpire Premier Cricket.

📺Live Streamed

Watch via Frogbox on YouTube and PlayCricket app. PlayHQ live scoring keeps everyone connected.

🥇1st & 2nd XI Divisions

Teams are graded into 1st and 2nd XI divisions.

🏆Awards & Trophies

Player of Match every game plus trophies, medals and awards — best batsman, bowler, fielder, most sixes and more.

👥1,600+ Players

100 teams took part in the Winter 2026 season.

📣Season Information

Where things stand in the Winter 2026 season, and how to hear about the next one.

Registrations closed

Winter 2026 is in its final rounds

Team registrations for Winter 2026 have closed and the season is now in its closing stages. The Saturday T20 competition has finished, Saturday T35 is into its finals, and Saturday T35 Non MYCA continues into September. Junior rounds run on alternate Sundays.
